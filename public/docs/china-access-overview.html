Why It Happens

This is a network problem, not an application problem

  1. Limited direct peering

    International cloud and CDN providers have little or no direct peering with the major Chinese ISPs, so cross-border traffic is pushed onto congested, indirect paths.

  2. Routing varies inside China

    Behaviour differs widely between regions and between ISPs, which is why performance can look acceptable in one city and unusable in another.

  3. MetaTrader is especially exposed

    Client terminals in China are left connecting to overseas access nodes across those same unpredictable paths, so latency and stability suffer most where it matters.

A dedicated China-facing layer is what turns this into stable, predictable performance for your traders. Your platform itself does not change.
