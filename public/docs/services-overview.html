Your Support Portal

New-domain setup and domain rotation, automated and run by your own team

Every client gets a dedicated support portal for the domain side of China access. Setting up a new domain and rotating traffic onto it become routine operations your team runs in minutes, instead of provider-specific engineering work.

Set up a new domain in minutes

Add the domain, pick or upload its certificate, and one Auto-config step provisions the acceleration path, security rules and certificate together and hands you the DNS record to point at.

Rotate to a new domain on demand

Move traffic to a fresh domain the same way, in minutes, with no ticket and no engineer in the loop.

Test from inside China, roll back in one click

Check any domain from Mainland China before and after a change. The live configuration is snapshotted before every change and restored with a single click if you need it.

In your own cloud account, fully audited

The portal runs against your own cloud account, so your credentials never leave your environment. Every action is logged, and read-only access is available for the wider team.
